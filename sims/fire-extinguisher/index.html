What do you grab?

The coloured band on each extinguisher tells you what's inside. Learn the colours and you can choose in a second.

Fight or get out?

Four fires, four workplaces. For each one, grab the right thing from the wall, or decide it's time to leave.

  • The wrong choice plays out, so you'll see why it's wrong.
  • With the right extinguisher: pull the pin, then hold the mouse down to spray. Aim at the base of the fire and sweep.

  • 1Paper, wood, fabric: water or foam.
  • 2Anything electrical: CO₂. Never water.
  • 3Burning fat: heat off, fire blanket. Never water.
  • 4Bigger than a bin, or spreading: raise the alarm and get out.
