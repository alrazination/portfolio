Every dot is a person

Six ordinary moments from a working week. Each one involves personal data: customers, colleagues, job applicants.

On the right, every person involved is a dot inside your company. Make the call, and watch where their data ends up.

  • Share personal data only with people who need it. Bulk email goes through the mailing tool.
  • Keep it in company systems, never personal email or USB sticks.
  • Access requests are free and due within one month. Check who's asking.
  • Tell your data protection lead about a breach straight away. The clock is 72 hours.
  • Marketing email needs consent. An unsubscribe link isn't consent.
  • Don't keep data longer than you need it. Data you delete can't leak.
